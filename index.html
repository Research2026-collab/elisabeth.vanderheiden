Research Project · 2025–2027

Modelling and Transfer of Content and Quality Criteria for Programme Planning in Adult Financial Education (MoTIf)

The MoTIf research project (2025–2027, funded by the BMBFSFJ) examines how adult financial education programmes can be planned and implemented more effectively.

The project develops criteria to support programme planning for planners, managers, educators and participants.

MoTIf brings together the expertise of two universities and four major practice partners: Humboldt-Universität zu Berlin (coordination), Justus-Liebig-Universität Gießen, Brandenburgischer Volkshochschulverband e. V. (BVV), Evangelische Erwachsenenbildung – Bundesverband e. V. / Comenius-Institut – Evangelische Arbeitsstätte für Erziehungswissenschaft e. V., Katholische Erwachsenenbildung Rheinland-Pfalz Landesarbeitsgemeinschaft e. V. (KEB RLP) on behalf of Katholische Erwachsenenbildung Deutschland, and Lernende Region – Netzwerk Köln e. V.

As a consortium partner, KEB RLP contributes experience and expertise from many years of work across adult education. Its work includes close cooperation with major public-benefit adult education providers and participation in academic discourse and educational practice. A resource- and potential-oriented approach is central to KEB RLP’s educational work.
